# Privacy Policy for Simple Media Organizer

Last Updated: October 23, 2026

Simple Media Organizer ("the Application") is an open-source, local-only desktop utility developed by nimidz. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
Simple Media Organizer does not collect, record, transmit, or share any personal data, sensitive personal information, or device data.
- **Local Files & Storage:** The Application only accesses files and directories on your local device that you explicitly select or choose to scan. All indexing, viewing, organizing, renaming, and moving of files happens 100% locally on your computer.
- **No Internet Access or Tracking:** The Application does not connect to external analytics services, telemetry engines, advertising networks, or third-party cloud servers.
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to register an account or provide any email address or credentials to use the Application.

## 2. Data Storage
Any preferences or settings you configure (such as custom category definitions, sort order, and view modes) are saved exclusively on your local machine using standard local application storage. No settings or user data are ever uploaded to any cloud server.

## 3. Third-Party Services
Simple Media Organizer does not use or integrate third-party data tracking, advertising SDKs, or analytics services. External links (such as to the GitHub repository or license page) are only opened when explicitly clicked by you in your default web browser.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Because the Application does not collect any personal information whatsoever, it does not collect information from children of any age.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected in this document with a revised "Last Updated" date.
